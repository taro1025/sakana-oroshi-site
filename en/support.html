FAQ

Saying "next" does nothing

Open Settings → Oroshi and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed. While listening, the steps screen shows "Say next, back or again". Recognition runs on your iPhone; on models that don't support it, tap anywhere on the screen to move on.

I changed phones and lost the paid fish

Go to Settings (the gear) → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

I'm left-handed

Turn on Settings → Left-handed to mirror every diagram.
