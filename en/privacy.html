Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Process Craft AI ("we") handles information in the iPhone app Oroshi (the "App").

Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information, device identifiers or usage data. There are no accounts, and no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Microphone and speech recognition

To let you move through the steps by voice, the App uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition running on your iPhone (on-device recognition). Audio and transcripts are never saved or sent. You can always tap the screen instead.

Data stored on your device

Your answers in the intro, your settings and which fish you have finished are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to our servers. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

The App describes cooking steps that use knives. Children should use it under adult supervision.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
